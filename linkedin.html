Why don’t you have a LinkedIn profile? Read why Close

The short version is that the incentives are misaligned. A professional network that centralizes employment history, relationships, and contact details creates an unusually valuable dataset for scraping, cross-referencing, and breach-based enrichment. The platform captures the upside; its users absorb most of the privacy risk.

The feed has the same incentive problem. It rewards reach, repetition, and self-promotion more reliably than accuracy or useful discussion, so engagement bait and spam crowd out the conversations I would actually want to read. I would rather publish a small page I control and spend my attention elsewhere.
